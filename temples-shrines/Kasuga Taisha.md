**Kasuga Taisha (Nara)**

Kasuga Taisha is Nara's most important shrine, sitting at the edge of Nara Park and the primeval Kasugayama forest. It's known for its hundreds of bronze and stone lanterns lining the approach paths — atmospheric even in daytime.

It fits naturally into the Nara Park loop with Todai-ji and the deer, so you'll probably walk through here anyway.

<img src="../images/kasuga_taisha_1.jpg" alt="Kasuga Taisha lantern-lined path in Nara" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Spring and autumn for the park and forest setting.
- February and August during the Mantoro lantern festivals, when all 3,000+ lanterns are lit at once.

<img src="../images/kasuga_taisha_lanterns.jpg" alt="Kasuga Taisha illuminated lanterns" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- The outer grounds are free; there's a fee for the inner sanctuary.
- Part of a UNESCO World Heritage designation shared with Todai-ji and nearby historic sites.
- See [Nara City](../locations/regions/5.%20Kansai/Nara/Nara%20City.md) for the full Nara day-trip plan.
