**Dazaifu Tenmangu (Fukuoka)**

Dazaifu Tenmangu is one of Japan's most important shrines dedicated to Tenjin, the deity of learning and scholarship. Students come here before exams — you'll see a lot of ema (wooden wish plaques) hanging everywhere.

It's a 40-minute train ride from Fukuoka and makes an easy half-day cultural side trip, combined with the Kyushu National Museum right next door.

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Late February to early March for plum blossoms — the shrine is famous for them.
- Autumn for comfortable walking and fewer crowds.

<img src="../images/dazaifu_tenmangu_ema.jpg" alt="Dazaifu Tenmangu wooden wish plaques" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- The route from the station is lined with shops selling umegae mochi (plum rice cake) — worth trying.
- Combine with [Kyushu National Museum](../museums/Kyushu%20National%20Museum.md) which is a 5-minute walk through a covered escalator tunnel from the shrine.
- See [Dazaifu](../locations/regions/8.%20Kyushu%20%26%20Okinawa/Fukuoka/Dazaifu.md) for transport and routing from Fukuoka.
