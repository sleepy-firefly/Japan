**Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto)**

Fushimi Inari is one of those places that genuinely looks like the photos — thousands of bright orange torii gates winding up a forested mountain south of Kyoto. It's dedicated to Inari, the Shinto deity of rice, business, and foxes.

You can walk 15 minutes to the main viewpoints, or go all the way to the top (about 2 hours up, 2 hours down) for much quieter trails and mountain views.

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round, but early morning in any season is the best call — crowds thin out fast past the first big gate clusters.
- Autumn (November) for foliage mixed with the orange gates.

<img src="../images/fushimi_inari_summit.jpg" alt="Fushimi Inari summit views" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Get there before 08:00 if you want relatively empty gate corridors — it fills up fast.
- Free to enter, 24 hours.
- The higher trails past Yotsutsuji viewpoint are much quieter than the base area.
