**Izumo Taisha (Shimane)**

Izumo Taisha is one of Japan's oldest and most important shrines, dedicated to Okuninushi-no-Mikoto — the deity associated with relationships and marriage. People come here from all over Japan to pray for good luck in love.

It's in a less-touristy part of Japan (Shimane Prefecture), which actually makes it feel more authentic and unhurried compared to the Kyoto/Nara shrine circuit.

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- May and October are comfortable for walking the grounds.
- October is especially significant — in the old lunar calendar, all Japan's deities are said to gather at Izumo Taisha this month.

<img src="../images/izumo_taisha_autumn.jpg" alt="Izumo Taisha in autumn" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- The main hall itself is off-limits but the surrounding complex and the massive shimenawa (rope) hanging inside the Kagura-den hall are the main attractions.
- Combine with Matsue (30–40 minutes away) for a good western Shimane day or overnight loop.
