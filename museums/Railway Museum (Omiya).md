The Railway Museum in Omiya is one of Japan's top transport museums, with historic rolling stock and rail technology exhibits.

It is a major family-friendly and enthusiast-friendly museum near Tokyo.

<img src="../images/railway_museum_omiya.jpg" alt="Railway Museum in Omiya" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round indoor destination.

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Combine with Bonsai Village for a full Omiya day.
