**National Science Museum - Paleontology Hall (Ueno, Tokyo)**

The paleontology section of the National Museum of Nature and Science is one of the best dinosaur displays in Tokyo, with an especially strong collection of Japanese finds alongside international specimens.

Perfect if you want serious dino time but also want to mix it into a broader Ueno museum day.

<img src="../images/national_museum_of_nature_and_science.jpg" alt="National Museum of Nature and Science paleontology exhibits" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round; indoor and especially useful on rainy or very hot days.

<img src="../images/national_science_paleontology_fossils.jpg" alt="Fossil displays in paleontology hall" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Allow 1-2 hours for the paleontology displays. Combines well with the broader museum exploration.
