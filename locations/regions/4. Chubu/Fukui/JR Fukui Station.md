**JR Fukui Station (Fukui City)**

JR Fukui Station is the main gateway for Fukui travel, connecting shinkansen and regional routes with city-center hotels, food, and onward buses.

It is the most practical base point for building day trips across Fukui Prefecture.

<img src="../../../../images/jr_fukui_station_1.jpg" alt="JR Fukui Station exterior and station plaza" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Best use case**

- First stop for luggage drop, route planning, and day-trip transfers.

<img src="../../../../images/jr_fukui_station_2.jpg" alt="JR Fukui Station platforms and train access" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Nearby highlights**

- Walkable food areas and business hotels.
- Easy transfer point to Echizen Railway services.

<img src="../../../../images/jr_fukui_station_3.jpg" alt="JR Fukui Station concourse and transfer area" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- If you are doing same-day side trips, check return trains before leaving the station area.
