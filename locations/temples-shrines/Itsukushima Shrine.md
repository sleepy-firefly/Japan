**Itsukushima Shrine (Miyajima)**

Itsukushima Shrine is built over the water on Miyajima Island, with its famous large torii gate standing in Hiroshima Bay. It's one of the most photographed spots in Japan and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The view at high tide — shrine buildings over the sea, torii glowing at sunset — is the classic shot. At low tide you can walk right up to the gate.





&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Any season works well; spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage add extra scenery.
- Late afternoon/sunset for the best torii lighting.



&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Short ferry ride from Miyajimaguchi (accessible from Hiroshima in about 30 minutes total).
- Deer roam freely on the island — they'll try to eat your map.
- Stay overnight to see it after day crowds leave: the evening atmosphere is very different.
