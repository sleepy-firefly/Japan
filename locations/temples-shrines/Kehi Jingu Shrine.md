**Kehi Jingu Shrine (Tsuruga, Fukui)**

Kehi Jingu is one of Fukui's most significant shrines, known for its large red torii and central role in Tsuruga's cultural events.

It is an easy and meaningful stop when building a Tsuruga-focused day route.



&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Spring and autumn for comfortable city-and-shrine walks.
- Festival dates for more ceremonial atmosphere.



&emsp;&emsp;**Why visit**

- Important historical shrine with strong local identity.
- Easy to combine with Tsuruga station area and waterfront planning.
