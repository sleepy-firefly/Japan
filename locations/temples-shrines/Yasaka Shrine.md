**Yasaka Shrine (Kyoto)**

Yasaka Shrine sits at the east end of Shijo-dori in Gion, which makes it one of the most accessible shrines in Kyoto — you basically walk straight off the main shopping street into it. It's open 24 hours and the lantern-lit grounds at night are some of the most atmospheric in Kyoto.

It's also the home base of [Gion Matsuri](../events/festivals/Gion%20Matsuri%20Festival.md), Kyoto's biggest festival, which runs through July.





&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- July is the main event — Gion Matsuri floats stage near and around the shrine.
- Evening visits year-round: the stone lanterns are lit after dark and the crowds thin out noticeably.



&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Free to enter, no closing time.
- Combine with a Gion evening walk — Hanamikoji, the old machiya streets, and Maruyama Park are all within a short walk.
- See [Gion](../locations/regions/5.%20Kansai/Kyoto/Kyoto%20City/Districts/Gion.md) for the full district guide.
