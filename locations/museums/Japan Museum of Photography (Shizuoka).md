**Japan Museum of Photography (Shizuoka)**

Japan Museum of Photography is a specialized museum focused entirely on photography art, history, and culture. It's a rewarding stop if you're interested in visual arts and you're in Shizuoka.

Great for a calm, focused museum day away from crowded Tokyo.



&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round; indoor destination.



&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Located in Shizuoka, easily accessible from Tokyo. Combine with local walking or nearby hot springs.
