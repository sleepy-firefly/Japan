**teamLab Borderless (Azabudai, Tokyo)**





teamLab Borderless is an immersive digital art experience designed as a flowing museum space without walls. Totally different feel from traditional museums — it's interactive and visually stunning.

Great for a unique art/tech crossover day if you want something completely different from conventional museum visits.



&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round; fully indoor and climate-controlled.

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Book tickets online in advance, especially for weekends. Allow 2-3 hours minimum for a meaningful visit.
