**Nagano Prefectural Dinosaur Museum (Nagano)**

Nagano Prefectural Dinosaur Museum is one of Japan's premier fossil and dinosaur museums, especially known for high-quality curation and Japanese paleontological finds.

Makes for an excellent day trip from Nagano city, and worth planning into a broader Nagano or alpine region visit.



&emsp;&emsp;**Best season/month**

- Year-round; indoor museum with comfortable climate regardless of season.



&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Best done as a dedicated museum day trip. Plan 2-3 hours for a thorough visit.
