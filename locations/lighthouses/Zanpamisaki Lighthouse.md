**Zanpamisaki Lighthouse**

Zanpamisaki Lighthouse is a lighthouse in Okinawa Prefecture, Japan. It is one of the country's tourable lighthouses, with interior access on designated open days.

<img src="../../images/zanpamisaki_lighthouse_1.jpg" alt="Zanpamisaki Lighthouse exterior view" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Why visit**

- Distinctive coastal setting that works well for photography and a short scenic stop.
- Tower access makes it more rewarding than an exterior-only lighthouse visit.

<img src="../../images/zanpamisaki_lighthouse_2.jpg" alt="Zanpamisaki Lighthouse interior or detail view" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

&emsp;&emsp;**Tour / access**

- Check opening days and admission before visiting.
- Weather and transport schedules can affect the experience.

&emsp;&emsp;**Practical note**

- Pair it with nearby coast roads, capes, or harbor stops.
