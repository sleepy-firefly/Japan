**Buses (City Bus and Highway Bus)**

Buses are useful for low-cost intercity travel and for areas where rail access is limited.

&emsp;&emsp;**Ticket options**

- Single ride city fare: fixed fare or distance fare depending on city.
- Prepaid IC card fare: same price or small discount in some networks.
- City day pass: common in tourist-heavy cities.
- Highway bus single ticket: one-way reserved seat.
- Highway bus round trip or multi-coupon book: offered on selected operators/routes.

&emsp;&emsp;**Typical price ranges by region**

- City bus single ride (major cities): about JPY 210 to 300.
- Kyoto and similar tourist cities day pass products (when sold): often around JPY 700 to 1,100.
- Tokyo to Osaka highway bus: roughly JPY 3,000 to 10,000 depending on day/night, seat class, and season.
- Osaka to Hiroshima highway bus: roughly JPY 3,500 to 6,500.
- Hokkaido and Kyushu long-distance buses: pricing varies widely by distance and demand.

&emsp;&emsp;**Multi-use value guidance**

- City day passes usually pay off after about 3 to 4 rides.
- Highway bus discount fares are often tied to advance purchase and limited seat inventory.
- Multi-coupon books can be worthwhile for repeated commuting between the same cities.

&emsp;&emsp;**Season and timing effects**

- Bus fares are more dynamic than rail on many long routes.
- Peak travel windows (Golden Week, Obon, New Year, holiday weekends) can push prices to upper ranges.
- Overnight buses can be cheaper than train + hotel, but comfort varies strongly by seat type.

&emsp;&emsp;**Booking and practical notes**

- Reserve highway buses early in peak periods.
- Confirm luggage allowance, restroom stops, and women-only seat policies where relevant.
- For remote sightseeing areas, check final return bus time before departure.
