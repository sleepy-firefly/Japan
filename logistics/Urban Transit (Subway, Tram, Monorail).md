**Urban Transit (Subway, Tram, Monorail)**

Urban transit is the core way to move inside large cities and many tourist districts.

&emsp;&emsp;**Ticket options**

- Single fare ticket: short-distance or occasional use.
- IC card pay-as-you-go: fastest option in most cities.
- One-day pass: often valid on subway only, city transit only, or combined networks.
- 48/72-hour tourist passes (city dependent): can be strong value for dense sightseeing days.

&emsp;&emsp;**Typical price ranges by city/region**

- Subway single ride in major metros: often around JPY 180 to 330.
- Tram single ride (where fixed fare): often around JPY 170 to 250.
- One-day city transit passes: often around JPY 600 to 1,200.
- Tokyo and Osaka have many operator-specific products; Kyoto, Sapporo, Fukuoka, Nagasaki, and Hiroshima also have practical local passes.

&emsp;&emsp;**Multi-use value guidance**

- One-day passes usually pay off after 3 to 5 rides depending on city and transfer pattern.
- 48/72-hour products are best when visiting multiple neighborhoods per day.
- Operator boundaries matter: a pass may not cover every private line.

&emsp;&emsp;**Season and timing effects**

- Base fares are mostly stable year-round.
- Crowding rises in blossom/foliage seasons and holiday periods, but fare level usually does not change much.
- Event days (festivals, concerts) can affect route convenience and transfer time.

&emsp;&emsp;**Booking and practical notes**

- Check pass coverage maps before buying.
- Keep one IC card for convenience if mixing operators.
- Last-train timing is important after evening events.
