**Domestic Flights**

Flights are best for very long distances such as Honshu to Hokkaido, Okinawa, or southern Kyushu.

&emsp;&emsp;**Ticket options**

- Standard one-way fare: flexible but usually expensive.
- Advance-purchase discount fare: cheaper, with stricter change/cancel rules.
- Round trip booking: can reduce average segment cost on some date combinations.
- Tourist or region-focused promotional fares: sometimes offered by major carriers and low-cost carriers.

&emsp;&emsp;**Typical price ranges by route/region (one-way)**

- Tokyo to Sapporo: about JPY 7,000 to 30,000+.
- Tokyo to Fukuoka: about JPY 7,000 to 28,000+.
- Tokyo to Naha (Okinawa): about JPY 8,000 to 35,000+.
- Osaka to Sapporo/Fukuoka/Okinawa: often competitive, with strong low-cost carrier variation.

&emsp;&emsp;**Multi-use value guidance**

- There is no universal domestic air pass equivalent to rail products for most travelers.
- Savings usually come from advance purchase and flexible departure times.
- For multi-city trips, open-jaw tickets can reduce backtracking and total cost.

&emsp;&emsp;**Season and timing effects**

- Flights show the largest seasonal price swings of any transport type.
- Highest prices: holiday peaks, school vacation periods, and weekends.
- Lowest prices: shoulder seasons and midweek off-peak departures.

&emsp;&emsp;**Booking and practical notes**

- Book early for holiday travel and Okinawa/Hokkaido routes.
- Include airport transfer time/cost in total trip planning.
- Check baggage fees on low-cost carriers before comparing final prices with rail.
