&emsp;&emsp;**How to choose quickly**

- Long intercity route with fixed schedule: trains first, flights second for very long distances.
- Flexible budget route or overnight move: highway bus.
- Island routes: ferry + local bus/rail.
- Big cities: subway/tram/metro day passes can save money if you make many short rides.

&emsp;&emsp;**Seasonality reminder**

- Golden Week (late Apr to early May), Obon (mid-Aug), and New Year are peak transport periods.
- Book reserved transport early in peak periods; prices and seat availability are less favorable.
