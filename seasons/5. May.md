**May (5月)**

May is one of the best months for travel in Japan. The weather is usually warm but not yet humid, skies are often clear, and the landscapes are full of fresh green leaves after spring.

<img src="../images/may_garden.jpg" alt="Japanese garden in May with fresh greenery" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

These are the things that make May so worth the trip:

* Golden Week falls at the beginning of May and is one of the busiest holiday periods in Japan, so trains, hotels, and famous attractions can become very crowded.

<img src="../images/may_golden_week_crowds.jpg" alt="Golden Week crowds at popular spots" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

* Outside of Golden Week, May is an excellent month for hiking and day trips; for major temple picks see the [Temple Index](../temples-shrines/Temple%20Index.md).

* Mountains, parks, and rural areas look especially beautiful with fresh spring greenery, making it a great time for scenic train rides and countryside travel.

<img src="../images/may_mountain_green.jpg" alt="Mountains with fresh green foliage in May" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />

* Late spring flowers such as wisteria, azaleas, and roses are also in season in many parts of the country.

<img src="../images/may_wisteria_flowers.jpg" alt="Wisteria and spring flowers in May" style="width: 80%; height: auto;" />
