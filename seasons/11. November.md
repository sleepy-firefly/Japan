**November (11月)**

November is one of the most beautiful months in Japan because autumn colors reach many famous gardens and temple areas; see the [Temple Index](../temples-shrines/Temple%20Index.md) for top options. The weather is usually cool and pleasant, making it one of the easiest months for sightseeing.



November surprised me more than almost any other month — here's what stood out:

* Maple leaves and ginkgo trees are at their best in many cities, especially around temple grounds and traditional gardens; see the [Temple Index](../locations/temples-shrines/Temple%20Index.md).



* November is a very comfortable month for walking-focused trips, with crisp air, low humidity, and many sunny days.

* This is also a good time to combine city travel with countryside or hot spring stays, especially in mountain areas with autumn scenery.



* Popular autumn foliage spots can still be very busy, so visiting early in the morning often makes the experience much more peaceful.
