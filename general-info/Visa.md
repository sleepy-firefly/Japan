**Visa Requirements for Japan (Tourist, under 90 days)**

---

&emsp;&emsp;**1. Poland**

Polish citizens are **visa-exempt** for Japan for tourism stays up to 90 days under the Japan-Poland visa exemption agreement. No visa application is required.

**What you need:**

- Valid Polish passport (valid for the duration of stay)
- Return or onward ticket
- Proof of sufficient funds for the stay
- Completed arrival card (filled on the plane or at immigration)

**At the border:**

- Immigration officer may ask about accommodation, itinerary, and available funds
- Tourist stamp grants up to 90 days; extension is generally not permitted for this category

**Important:**

- Working on a tourist entry is prohibited
- 90-day limit applies per entry; re-entry does not automatically reset the counter in all cases

**Official references:**

- Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs: https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/
- Polish MFA Japan info: https://www.gov.pl/web/dyplomacja

---

&emsp;&emsp;**2. Belarus**

Belarusian citizens are **not visa-exempt** and must apply for a Japanese tourist visa before travel.

**Visa type:** Single-entry tourist visa (up to 90 days stay within 3 months of issue)

**Embassy of Japan in Belarus:**

- Address: 23 Bedy Street, Minsk 220004, Republic of Belarus
- Phone: +375 17 203-62-33
- Email: consular@mn.mofa.go.jp (consular section)
- Website: https://www.by.emb-japan.go.jp/
- Working hours: confirm current hours on the official website before visiting

**Application process:**

1. Complete the visa application form (available on the embassy website or in person)
2. Gather required documents (see below)
3. Submit in person at the embassy or via an authorised visa agency
4. Wait for processing (typically 5 to 7 business days for standard processing)
5. Collect passport with visa

**Required documents:**

- Valid passport (must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended stay)
- Completed visa application form (one per person)
- One passport-size photo (4 × 3 cm, plain background, recent)
- Confirmed return flight itinerary
- Proof of accommodation for the trip (hotel bookings or host invitation)
- Proof of sufficient financial means (bank statement for the past 3 months)
- Travel insurance covering the full period (recommended; may be required)
- If employed: letter from employer confirming leave approval and employment status
- If self-employed or student: relevant supporting documents

**Visa fee:**

- Single-entry: approximately USD 35 (fee is set in USD; confirm current rate on the embassy website as it can change)
- Multiple-entry visas may be available depending on travel history and purpose

**Processing time:**

- Standard: 5 to 7 working days
- Apply well in advance of travel; do not buy non-refundable flights before the visa is confirmed

**Official references:**

- Embassy of Japan in Belarus: https://www.by.emb-japan.go.jp/
- Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (visa): https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/

---

**Note:** Visa rules and fees can change. Always verify current requirements directly with the embassy or MOFA website before planning travel.
